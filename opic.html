핵심: 오픽에서 형식 자체를 외우는 게 목적이 아니라, 자주 쓰는 동사가 어떤 문장 뼈대를 요구하는지 익혀두는 게 목적. 특히 2형식의 형용사, 4형식의 사람+것, 5형식의 목적어+상태/행동을 익혀두면 문장이 훨씬 안정적입니다.

1형식 · S + V

주어 + 자동사

S + V (+ 장소 / 시간 / 방법)
go — 가다
I usually go there on weekends.
come — 오다
My friends sometimes come over to my place.
arrive — 도착하다
We arrived at the hotel late at night.
stay — 머무르다
I usually stay home during my vacation.
happen — 일어나다
Something unexpected happened during the trip.
work — 작동하다 / 일하다
My computer suddenly stopped working.
같이 외우기: go to, arrive at/in, stay at/home
2형식 · S + V + C

주어 + 상태 동사 + 보어

S + be / feel / seem / look / sound / become + 형용사·명사
be — ~이다
The place was really quiet and comfortable.
feel — ~하게 느끼다
I felt really relaxed there.
seem — ~처럼 보이다
It seemed a little crowded at first.
look — ~해 보이다
The ocean looked beautiful.
sound — ~하게 들리다
That sounds like a good idea.
become / get — ~하게 되다
It got really busy in the afternoon.
오픽 핵심: I felt comfortable.처럼 뒤에 형용사를 바로 붙이는 구조를 많이 씀.
3형식 · S + V + O

주어 + 타동사 + 목적어

S + V + 명사 / 대명사 / 동명사 / to부정사
like / love / enjoy — 좋아하다
I really enjoy watching movies at home.
prefer — 더 선호하다
I prefer quiet places.
use — 사용하다
I use my smartphone every day.
watch / play / visit — 보다 / 하다 / 방문하다
I often watch movies and play games after dinner.
remember — 기억하다
I clearly remember that trip.
need / want — 필요로 하다 / 원하다
I wanted some time to relax.
choose / buy / find — 고르다 / 사다 / 찾다
I chose a hotel near the station.
4형식 · S + V + IO + DO

사람에게 무언가를

S + V + 사람 + 것
give — 주다
The staff gave me some information.
tell — 말해주다
My friend told me the address.
show — 보여주다
He showed me a nice restaurant nearby.
send — 보내주다
The airline sent me a message.
ask — 부탁하다 / 묻다
I asked the staff a few questions.
offer — 제공하다
They offered me another room.
문제 해결 롤플레이에 특히 유용: tell me, give me, show me, send me, offer me.
5형식 · S + V + O + C

목적어를 ~하게 만들다 / 여기다

S + V + 목적어 + 형용사 / 명사 / 동사원형·to부정사
make + O + 형용사/동사원형 — ~하게 만들다
It makes me feel comfortable.
Technology makes my life easier.
keep + O + 형용사 — ~한 상태로 유지하다
Having a routine keeps me organized.
find + O + 형용사 — ~가 ...하다고 느끼다
I find the app very useful.
call + O + 명사 — ~라고 부르다
People call this area a hot spot.
want + O + to V — ~가 ...하기를 원하다
I wanted my friend to come with me.
ask + O + to V — ~에게 ...해 달라고 부탁하다
I asked the staff to check my reservation.
help + O + (to) V — ~가 ...하도록 돕다
AI helps me find information quickly.

PROBLEM SOLVING

문제 해결 공통 뼈대

stay calm → check the situation → ask for help → solve the urgent problem first → prevent it next time
First, I would try to stay calm.
Then, I would check what options I have.
If I couldn’t solve it myself, I would ask the staff for help.
I would deal with the most urgent problem first.
After that, I would make sure the same thing doesn’t happen again.
